Engine, privacy & credits

WebXR uses device hit tests on horizontal surfaces. The 8th Wall fallback uses tracked rays against an estimated horizontal ground plane, not surface detection. No persistent physical anchors. 8th Wall scripts and engine chunks load from jsDelivr after Start AR; network access is required. Envision adds no analytics or camera uploads. Third-party engine and speech behavior is outside Envision’s control.
